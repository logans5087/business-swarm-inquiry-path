# Business Swarm — Public Inquiry-Path Sprint

An AI-assisted website review and copy package for small service businesses.

## What you receive

For a **$350 USD pilot**, we review your homepage, contact page and up to three agreed service pages. You receive:

- An evidence-linked audit separating observed facts from hypotheses.
- A prioritized list of inquiry-path clarity improvements.
- Proposed calls to action, contact instructions and FAQ copy for the agreed pages.
- An implementation checklist and one consolidated revision round.

Delivery target: **14 calendar days after written scope acceptance and receipt of required public URLs and business-process confirmations**. No access to your customer records or account passwords is needed. We do not publish changes or contact your customers.

The pilot is an assessment and draft-copy service, not a promise of leads, rankings, bookings or revenue. AI agents prepare the work; the business must confirm operational facts and approve copy before using it.

## Small sample: a public plumbing website

An independent review of a Raleigh plumbing business's public homepage and contact page found:

1. A repeated service-request action alongside a telephone route. The button label alone did not describe the online request's next step.
2. Weekday office hours and after-hours emergency availability appeared together with general form-response wording. The form text did not give a specific response-time commitment.

These facts do **not** establish missed inquiries, slow staff response or customer confusion.

**Proposed improvement:** distinguish routine written requests from urgent telephone requests, and tell visitors what information to provide. For example:

> For routine service requests, describe the issue, property location and best callback number. For urgent or after-hours needs, use the telephone route confirmed by the business.

This is proposed copy, not a tested conversion result. Any emergency wording must be checked against the actual intake process before publication.
